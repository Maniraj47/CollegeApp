# Product Requirements Document: That College Hub ## Project Overview **That College Hub** is a campus-exclusive social and marketplace platform designed specifically for verified university students. It aims to foster a safe, fun, and authentic campus community by combining social networking with a secure student-to-student marketplace. ## Target Audience - Verified students from Indian colleges (IITs, NITs, Anna University, etc.). - Students looking to connect with peers, stay updated on campus events, and safely buy/sell items or services. ## Visual Identity & Design Principles - **Style:** Modern Material You (Android native feel). - **Color Palette:** Vibrant college-themed (Blues, Whites, Oranges). - **Vibe:** Safe, fun, authentic, and energetic. - **Key UI Elements:** Smooth animations, intuitive navigation, high contrast for accessibility, and consistent iconography. ## Core Features & Screens ### 1. Onboarding Flow - **Splash Screen:** Branded intro. - **College Selection:** Searchable dropdown for Indian institutions. - **Verification:** .edu email or ID verification (simulated zk-proof with OTP). - **Profile Setup:** Name, year, branch, bio, and photo. ### 2. Main Navigation (Bottom Nav Bar) - **Home (Feed):** Real-time posts (text, images, polls, events). - **Directory:** Searchable list of students with 'Connect' functionality and filters. - **Marketplace:** Grid listing for items and services (notes, tutoring). - **Chat:** Real-time one-on-one and group messaging. - **Profile:** Personal dashboard with post history and settings. ### 3. Key Functionalities - **Marketplace Detail & Escrow:** Detailed item views with a secure milestone-based escrow wallet (30/40/30 split). - **Wallet Screen:** UPI deposits, balance tracking, and transaction history. - **Engagement:** Likes, comments, shares, and a post creation modal with a photo picker. - **Security & Privacy:** Verification badges, privacy-focused data handling, and push notifications. - **Accessibility:** High contrast support and voiceover compatibility. ## Technical Considerations - Dark/Light mode support. - Offline caching for the home feed. - Responsive design for various Android screen sizes.
